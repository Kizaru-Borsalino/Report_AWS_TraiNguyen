### Project Overview

During the internship, I worked on **JobGo**, a cloud-based recruitment platform that serves three main user groups: **candidates**, **companies**, and **administrators**. The system allows candidates to build profiles, manage resumes, and receive **AI Matching** suggestions; companies can publish job posts, review applicants, and update hiring statuses; administrators are responsible for moderation, master-data governance, and platform operations.

The production-oriented AWS architecture of JobGo is defined as follows:

- The **React frontend** is built and delivered through **Amazon S3 + Amazon CloudFront**.
- The **FastAPI backend** is containerized and deployed on **Amazon ECS Fargate** behind an **Application Load Balancer**.
- Relational data is stored in **Amazon RDS for PostgreSQL**.
- Candidate resumes are stored in a **private Amazon S3 bucket** and accessed through **presigned URLs**.
- Operational monitoring relies on **Amazon CloudWatch Logs** and basic alarms.
