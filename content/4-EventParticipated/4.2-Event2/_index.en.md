## Activities

During the event, I observed product demonstrations and idea pitches from multiple teams. The projects addressed real-world problems across different domains such as food service, finance, video games, transportation, and smart devices.

Each team presented the problem they wanted to solve, how their AI system worked, the technologies used, the feasibility of the product, and the business value that the solution could deliver. This helped me observe how a technology idea can be developed into a prototype and presented to judges.

## Highlighted Projects

### KFC BOT

**KFC BOT** is an AI agent that allows customers to search for menu items, check vouchers, confirm orders, and complete KFC purchases directly inside Zalo or Messenger without switching to another application. The system was built on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore and can process the main steps of an order in about three to five seconds.

This project showed me that AI is not limited to answering questions. It can also perform a sequence of tasks and help customers complete a transaction. KFC BOT achieved the top position in the **Built with AWS** category.

### Agamotto

**Agamotto** is a solution that helps video game companies evaluate different versions of characters, costumes, or in-game items before official release. From an initial design request, the system can generate a 2D image version, convert it into a 3D model, and use multiple AI agents with different user characteristics to simulate reactions to each design.

The system then summarizes the results into a report to help the design team choose a better option. This project won first place in the **VNG Games** category and helped me understand how AI can support market research, customer-behavior analysis, and product-development decisions.

### Cortex Sentinel

In the finance domain, **Cortex Sentinel** was developed as a compliance support center for anti-money laundering. Instead of requiring specialists to write suspicious-transaction detection rules in a programming language, the system allows them to describe requirements in natural language.

The AI reads previous alert data, suggests detection rules, and sends them back for specialist review and approval. The solution aims to reduce false alerts, save case-processing time, and still keep humans as the final decision makers.

### Elemental Arena

In the Robotics and Physical AI category, **Elemental Arena** combines AI software with a small physical device. Users can create an AI creature on a web platform and then use voice commands to let it interact or battle with another user's device.

Besides entertainment, the device can also act as a personal assistant for calendars, emails, and reminders. This project helped me realize that Agentic AI is not only a chatbot on a screen; it can also be integrated into physical devices to create new interactive experiences.

### Other Practical Ideas

Besides the award-winning projects, the event introduced many highly applicable ideas. **Fast Food Operations Copilot** is an operations assistant for fast-food chains. It can forecast sales by store and time period, detect abnormal data, identify possible causes, and simulate the results of corrective actions before managers make decisions.

Another project, **Tasco Whisperer**, supports Vietnamese map search. It can understand incomplete, abbreviated, or accent-missing Vietnamese queries, infer the user's intent, and rank suitable locations.

## Lessons Learned

Through the presentations, I realized that successful projects do not necessarily need too many features. More importantly, they must identify the right problem, build a clear solution, and demonstrate real practical value.

I also learned how teams present a technology project in a logical sequence: introducing the problem, proposing the solution, demonstrating the product, explaining the system architecture, and answering questions from judges. These lessons helped me better understand the process of turning an idea into a prototype while improving my analytical thinking, observation skills, and ability to evaluate the applicability of a technology solution.
